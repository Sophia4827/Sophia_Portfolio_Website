About Me

I'm a data scientist and analyst finishing my MS in Data Analytics at Binghamton University (GPA 3.93), where I also completed my BA in Psychology with a minor in Digital and Data Studies.

My work spans machine learning, statistical modeling, and data visualization, from building computer vision pipelines and audio classifiers to causal inference in healthcare analytics. I'm passionate about not just finding patterns in messy, real-world data, but transforming it into clear, actionable insights that can drive better decisions for teams, organizations, and the people they serve.
